---
title: "Integrated terminal (Forge)"
description: "Keep shell sessions and connected coding assistants beside your workspace."
---

Forge brings a real terminal into the desktop app, so your command-line work can sit beside the ideas, references, and notes that explain it. Open **Terminal** from the app rail.

## Arrange your sessions

Use **+** to open another tab. Double-click a tab to rename it.

Drag tabs to reorder them, move them between panels, or drop them in empty space to create another panel. Move and resize panels within the FlowJoe window. Maximize expands a panel inside that window.

## Keep work running

Minimizing a panel keeps its shells running. Closing the main panel hides it and preserves its sessions for reopening.

Closing a detached panel or an individual tab ends its shells. Quitting or reloading FlowJoe also ends sessions.

## Connect a coding assistant

Enable **Settings → Joe AI → Agent Bridge (MCP)**, then run your connected assistant in Forge or another terminal. The bridge runs in FlowJoe independently of the shell.

Forge is a normal terminal: commands use your account’s permissions, and can change files just as they can in another terminal app. Agent Bridge approval controls apply to changes requested through FlowJoe’s bridge, rather than every command you type.

You can also use an installed tool as [Joe’s brain](/joe/byob). In that case you talk to Joe in his chat panel; you do not need to open Forge or keep a terminal conversation running. An interactive assistant in Forge and Joe’s conversation have separate histories.

See [Agent Bridge](/joe/agent-bridge) for connection and approval settings. Terminal sessions are a desktop feature; they are unavailable in standalone browsers and shared guest sessions.
