title: "Image Editor"
description: "Edit a picture in a full editor and save the result as a new version of its slot."
---

**Edit Image** opens a picture from a slot in a full image editor right inside the gallery. Whatever you save is added as a **new version on top of the slot**: the original stays underneath in the slot's [stack](/gallery/stacks), and **Undo** puts it back.

The editor comes with FlowJoe: there is nothing to download or set up, and it works offline. **Crop Image** in the same menu stays the quickest way to just trim a picture.

---

## Opening the editor

1. Right-click a picture slot.
2. Choose **Edit Image › Open Editor…** (just under **Crop Image**).

**Edit Image** is greyed out for things it can't edit, the same ones **Crop Image** can't: a missing or placeholder picture, a video or a document, or a slot you can't change.

The editor opens PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, HEIC and SVG pictures (an SVG drawing is saved back as a PNG). For other kinds (an AVIF photo, for example) FlowJoe says so instead of opening it; **Crop Image** still works on them.

The editor opens straight away on the picture you picked.

## Quick fixes

Below **Open Editor…** the **Edit Image** menu has one-click fixes that work without opening the editor:

| Quick fix | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto-tone** | Balances the picture's light and contrast automatically. |
| **Rotate Left** / **Rotate Right** | Turns the picture a quarter turn. |
| **Flip Horizontal** | Mirrors the picture left to right. |
| **Sharpen** | Sharpens the details. |
| **Resize…** | Asks for a new size first (see below). |

Each one is saved like **Save as New Version**: the result goes on top of the slot, the original stays underneath in the [stack](/gallery/stacks), and **Undo** in the message that appears (for example **Sharpened. Saved as a new version in slot 1**) puts the slot back as it was. A JPEG picture stays a JPEG; everything else is saved as a PNG.

- A quick fix takes a few seconds (the editor starts in the background first; large photos take longer). A small message at the edge of the window shows it's working, for example **Sharpening cafe-front.jpg…**, and goes away when it's done. You can keep using FlowJoe meanwhile, or switch to another app: the quick fix carries on while FlowJoe's window is in the background.
- One quick fix runs at a time. Asking for another while one is running tells you to try again when it's done.
- While the editor screen is open, quick fixes wait: close the editor first. **Open Editor…** also waits while a quick fix is still running.
- If a quick fix can't be done, FlowJoe says so and the picture isn't changed.

### Resize…

**Resize…** opens a small window with **Width** and **Height** in pixels and the picture's current size below them.

- The link button between the fields keeps the proportions: change one side and the other follows. Click it to change the sides separately.
- **Enter** or **Resize** applies the size; **Cancel** or **Esc** closes the window without changing anything.
- Sizes are whole pixels. **Resize** stays off while the size is the picture's current size, and the window says why when a size can't be used (for example 0, or far too large for the editor).

## The editor screen

The editor takes the place of the gallery, like **Version History** does. The title row shows **Edit Image**, the picture's name, its slot and node, then **Advanced**, **Save as New Version** and **✕**.

Below it:

- **Tools** down the left: **Move**, **Crop**, Select, Brush, Remove spots, Text, Shapes, then Pick color, Zoom and Pan. Hover a tool to see its name and shortcut key; with the screen's buttons in focus, pressing that key picks the tool (it never triggers the tree's own one-letter shortcuts). The editor starts on **Move**.
- **A toolbar row** across the top: **Undo** and **Redo**, then the options of the tool you picked (or a one-line hint about what it does), and **Compare** on the right.
- **A status row** under the picture: its size, color space (sRGB), zoom, **−** / **+** and **Fit**.
- **The panel** on the right, with three tabs: **Adjust**, **Filters** and **Layers**.

Undo and Redo also bring the sliders back to match the picture, including an undo you type inside the picture (Cmd/Ctrl+Z).

### Crop and Text

- **Crop** shows a frame on the picture with a rule-of-thirds grid, like **Crop Image**. **Ratio** (Original, Free, 1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, 9:16 Story) reshapes the frame, **Straighten** turns it (up to 45° either way), and **W** / **H** show the frame's size. The other options rotate and flip the whole picture.
- **Text**: set the font, weight, size, color and alignment, then click the picture to type: the text starts in that style. Pick a text layer to change its style afterwards; **Shadow** needs a text layer picked.

A crop frame or text you're still typing is finished automatically when you save or compare, so what you see is what gets saved. In the rare case the editor doesn't finish it in time, nothing is saved and FlowJoe tells you; choose **Save as New Version** again.

### Adjust

The panel on the right holds the everyday adjustments:

| Group | Sliders |
|---|---|
| Light | Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows |
| Color | Warmth, Tint, Vibrance, Saturation |
| Detail | Sharpen, Clarity, Noise |

- While you drag a slider you see a quick preview; when you let go, the editor works the change into the picture itself. On a large photo that can take a moment.
- Sliders stay changeable until you save. **Reset** puts all of them back to zero.
- **Auto-tone** balances the picture's light and contrast automatically. It works on the untouched picture, so use it before moving the sliders.

### Filters

Ready-made looks: **Original**, **Warm morning**, **Soft film**, **Crisp**, **Black & white** and **Faded**. Click one to apply it, and use **Amount** to make it stronger or softer. **Original** removes the look. A look works alongside your Adjust sliders; it doesn't change them.

### Layers

The picture's layers, top to bottom. Click a layer to pick it, use the eye to hide or show it, and set its **Opacity** and **Blend** underneath. Drag a layer up or down to reorder it; a line shows where it will land. The buttons add a **new layer**, **duplicate** the picked one or **delete** it (FlowJoe asks first; **Undo** in the editor brings it back).

The picture itself is the bottom layer and is locked (it shows a lock): it can't be moved, duplicated or deleted, because it is what **Save as New Version** saves. Your look and your Adjust sliders show just above it as **Look** and **Adjustments**; their eyes hide or show them.

### Compare

**Compare** shows the original and your edit side by side, split by a line you can drag. Press it again, pick a tool or switch tabs to go back to editing.

### Advanced

Turn on **Advanced** to use the editor's full set of tools and panels (Curves, Levels, brushes, layer styles and more), in the editor's own layout; FlowJoe's tools, toolbar row, status row and panel step aside. **Save as New Version**, **Advanced** and **✕** stay where they are. Turn it off to go back, with the tool you had picked.

## Saving

**Save as New Version** applies your edits to the picture at full size. Large photos can take several seconds; FlowJoe shows that it's working and you can keep using the rest of FlowJoe. When it's done the editor closes and the slot shows the edited picture on top, with the original underneath. **Undo** in the message that appears puts the slot back as it was.

A JPEG picture is saved as a JPEG; everything else is saved as a PNG.

## Closing without saving

**✕** or **Esc** closes the editor. If you have unsaved edits, FlowJoe asks first: **Save as New Version**, **Discard**, or **Cancel** (which keeps you in the editor). Clicking another node in the tree (also with Shift or Cmd/Ctrl), opening **History**, deleting the node, importing or switching the Flow, restoring a version, and quitting or reloading FlowJoe ask too. A leave you ask for while a save is running waits for the save and then goes ahead.

<Note>
With **Advanced** on, or with **Crop** or **Text** picked, **Esc** inside the picture belongs to the tool (it cancels the crop frame or ends the typing); use **✕** to close.
</Note>

## If the editor stops

The editor runs separately from the rest of FlowJoe. If it stops (it can run out of memory on a very large photo), only the editor is affected: FlowJoe shows **The editor stopped. Your picture wasn't changed.** Your unsaved edits in the editor are lost. Choose **Reopen Editor** to start again on the same picture, or **Close**.

## Guests

People you've invited to a shared Flow in their browser use your copy of the editor: they never install anything themselves.

- A guest who can crop a picture gets **Edit Image ›** with **Open Editor…** and every quick fix, exactly as you do. The editor comes from your computer the first time they open it in their visit (about 12 MB) and then starts from what their browser already has.
- If you're on an older version of FlowJoe that didn't include the editor yet, they see **Edit Image** greyed out, without its arrow, with the line **The owner hasn't added the image editor yet.** under it.
- A guest who can only view the Flow can't crop, so they can't edit pictures either.
- What a guest saves goes into your Flow the same way as their **Crop Image** does: a new version on top of the slot, the original underneath. Changing a picture someone else added follows the invitation's **removal** permission, as Crop does: without it, FlowJoe keeps the picture as it is and tells the guest **Unable to remove item**, with **Ask (your name) to remove it or grant removal permission.**
- If someone replaces or removes the picture while it's open in the editor, a quick fix or **Crop Image**, FlowJoe doesn't save and says **The edit wasn't saved: the picture changed while the edit was open.** In the editor your edits stay on screen.
- The editor works in current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. A browser that can't draw it (it needs WebGL 2) shows **This browser can't run the image editor** instead; the picture isn't changed and **Crop Image** still works.
- In the browser, a quick fix keeps running only while the guest's tab is open in front: browsers slow down tabs that aren't being looked at, so a quick fix started just before switching tabs may take longer or not finish. The picture is never changed unless the fix finishes.

## Settings

**Settings › Gallery › Image Editor** says the editor is built in and shows its version (for example **Built in · version 0.15.1**). There is nothing to download or remove. The card appears in the desktop app for the Flow's owner; guests and the browser version don't see it.
