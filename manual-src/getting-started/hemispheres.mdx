## Left — structure

The tree is hierarchical and sequential. It likes order, categorization, parent–child relationships, and knowing where everything belongs.

Use it when you need:

- Project maps and branching plans
- People, chapters, milestones, research threads
- Clear place for a body of work

## Right — references

The gallery is a wide, visual canvas. It thrives on association, imagery, notes on media, color, mood, and seeing many things side-by-side without forcing every asset into its own tree node.

Use it when you need:

- Reference boards and mood collections
- Stacks of variants (takes, versions, exports)
- Documents, links, and workbenches living *inside* a place in the tree

## Choose a different node

Click **Menu & service** in the same tree and the gallery changes to that node’s references. The brand artwork stays with **Brand direction**; the menu draft belongs to **Menu & service**.

<Frame caption="The same Flow after choosing Menu & service. The highlighted node and the gallery now point to the menu work.">
  <img src="/images/getting-started/daybreak-menu-selection.png" alt="A close crop of the real app with Menu & service selected in the Daybreak café tree and its opening-menu draft visible in the gallery." />
</Frame>

## The divider

Drag the vertical divider to give more room to structure or more room to the gallery. That control is part of the product philosophy: you decide the balance for the moment you are in.
