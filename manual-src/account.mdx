Your FlowJoe account lives at **[account.flowjoe.app](https://account.flowjoe.app)**. Your Flows never leave your computer because of it: the account holds only your sign-in, profile, trial and signed-in devices.

<Note>
Account sign-in is in early access. If your email is not yet enabled, the sign-in page will not be able to send you a code.
</Note>

## Signing in

1. Open [account.flowjoe.app](https://account.flowjoe.app), enter your email address and choose **Email me a code**.
2. Open the newest FlowJoe email and type its six-digit code on the sign-in page.

There is no password to remember: every sign-in uses a fresh code sent to your inbox. Your first sign-in creates your account. Keep codes private — never send a code to another person or enter it on a page someone sent you.

### If the code does not work

- Check that you typed all six digits from the **newest** email. Requesting a new code makes older ones useless.
- Sending can be briefly limited if you ask for many codes in a row; wait a minute and try again.
- If the sign-in request has ended, choose **Start again** to begin a fresh one.

## Your account overview

After signing in you see your account overview: the email you are signed in with, your current access and sharing address, and how many computers are signed in. If your account has a plan, **Current access** shows it — for example **Pro** with **Sharing on** and its end date (or "No end date"). If your account owns a sharing address, **Your sharing address** shows it — for example `yourname.flowjoe.app` — marked **Active**. Details that are not available yet appear as a dash.

Use the menu on the left to move between **Overview**, **Devices** and **Security**; they switch instantly without reloading the page. **Plan & Passes** and **Team** are marked **Soon** and are not available yet.

## Signed-in computers and browsers

**Devices** lists the computers signed in to your account. The list stays current on its own while the page is open, so a computer signed in elsewhere appears without reloading.

- **Sign out** next to a computer ends that computer's account access. Its Flows stay on that computer.
- **Sign out all computers** ends account access on every computer and keeps this browser signed in.

If your last email code is more than ten minutes old, FlowJoe first asks for a fresh code. The code alone does not remove anything — you confirm again afterwards.

## Security

**Security** shows the email you sign in with, links to support and to [how your account data is handled](/account-privacy).

- **Change email**: enter your new address, then confirm the code sent to your current inbox and the different code sent to your new inbox. The change happens only after both confirmations. Use the new address next time you sign in.
- **Sign out everywhere**: after a confirmation, ends FlowJoe account access on every computer and every browser, including this one. Your Flows stay on your computers. If the sign-in service cannot confirm its part, the result says so — FlowJoe account access is still ended.

## Signing out of this browser

**Sign out** (top right of your account page) ends only the current browser session. Your other browsers and computers stay signed in. To use a different account, sign out and sign in again with the other email.

## Deleting your account

On **Security**, choose **Delete account…**, type the email you sign in with, and confirm. If your last email code is more than ten minutes old, FlowJoe first asks for a fresh code, then brings you back to the confirmation — the code alone never deletes anything.

Deleting your account:

- signs out every computer and browser and ends sharing from this account;
- removes your profile, sign-ins, computers, plan and trial history;
- frees your sharing address immediately, so anyone can choose it;
- keeps only a minimal, unlinked record that your email and computer already used a trial, so a new account doesn't get a second one.

The Flows on your computers are not affected. If you have paid time left, contact support before deleting. Deletion can't be undone; you can create a new account later with the same email.

## Signing in from the FlowJoe app

In the FlowJoe app, click your avatar (top right) and choose **Sign in**, or open **Settings › Account** and choose **Sign in with your browser**. FlowJoe opens a short sign-in page in your web browser. If that browser isn't already signed in, enter your email and the 6-digit code. Check that the code shown matches the one in the app, then approve. You're returned to the app, signed in. The first code creates your account, just like on the website.

The app stays signed in until you sign out or remove that computer under **Devices**. On a computer without secure storage, FlowJoe shows **Signed in until FlowJoe closes** and you sign in again after closing it. **Manage account** in the avatar menu opens this website.

### Starting your trial

If your email and computer haven't used a trial yet, **Settings › Account** offers **Try Pro free for 7 days**. Choose your sharing name and confirm. No card is needed, and the trial ends without a charge.

### Sharing from your address

With sharing access (Pro, a trial, a Project Pass or Team), the avatar menu shows your plan and **Sharing as yourname.flowjoe.app**, and FlowJoe sets up your sharing address on that computer automatically — there is nothing to configure. Press **Share** and turn sharing on to let guests in. When your address is set up on this computer, the Share window starts with **Sharing access**: your plan, your address, this computer and how many guests can edit. Signing out stops any share that is running.

## Not available yet

- Changing your display name or language.
- **Plan & Passes** (plans and billing) and **Team** — shown as **Soon** in the menu.
- Choosing or changing your sharing address from the website.

These will be documented here when they become available.
