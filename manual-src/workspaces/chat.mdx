---
title: "Chat"
description: "Talk with the people you share a Flow with: one group conversation for everyone, and private messages between two people."
---

While you share a Flow, you and your guests can talk to each other in FlowJoe. There is one group conversation, **Everyone**, and a private conversation between any two people. Chat is people only: Joe does not read it, and it is separate from your Joe conversations.

Chat is on unless you turn it off in the Share window (see [Chat with each other](/workspaces/sharing-and-presenting#chat-with-each-other)).

## Open Chat

- Choose **Chat** (the speech bubbles) on the left rail or in the header, right after Joe (the robot). Guests have it while the Flow is shared with Chat on. As the owner you always have it in the desktop app, even with no guests and sharing off, so you can read the Flow's saved group chat, and your own private conversations, at any time.
- Or press **Cmd+Shift+X** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Shift+X** (Windows and Linux). The same keys close it.

Chat opens in the right-hand column that Joe also uses. Whichever of the two the column is showing is highlighted, on the rail and in the header. If Joe is open, Chat slides over Joe and Joe waits underneath, just as you left it: choose Joe (the robot) to switch back to Joe, or close Chat. If Joe is closed, Chat opens the column on its own. Press **Escape** or the **×** to close Chat. The speech bubbles always mean chatting with people; the robot always means Joe — even Joe's own **Chat** tab shows the robot.

A green count on the **Chat** button, and on each conversation at the top of the panel, shows how many messages you haven't read yet. Someone who has sent you a private message you haven't read also gets a green count on their picture at the top of the window.

When a private message arrives while Chat is closed (or while you're in another conversation), a small notice shows who it's from and what they wrote. Choose **Reply** to open that conversation, or **Dismiss**. Messages in Everyone only raise the count; they never pop up.

## From someone's profile card

Point at (or click) anyone's picture at the top of the window to see their card. Choose **Message** on the card to open Chat straight to your private conversation with them.

If the owner has turned off messages between guests, another guest's card says so instead; you can still write in Everyone and message the owner. If the owner has muted you in Chat, the card says that instead: you can still read along and message the owner.

As the owner, a guest's card also has a mute button (a bell with a line through it). **Mute in Chat** stops that guest writing in Everyone without removing them from the Flow; they can still read along and message you. Choose it again to unmute.

## Conversations

The row at the top of the panel lists **Everyone** first, then the people in the Flow. Choose one to open that conversation.

- **Everyone**: everyone in the shared Flow sees it.
- **A person**: a private conversation between the two of you.

If the owner has turned off messages between guests, guests can still write in Everyone and message the owner; other guests' names are greyed out.

Each conversation keeps its own unsent words: switch to another conversation and back, and what you started is still there.

As the owner, your private conversations with a guest stay in the list after they leave, so you can read them back; you can write again when they return.

When sharing is off, the owner can still open **Chat** to read the Flow's saved group conversation (a Flow with none says **No messages yet**), and each private conversation kept on this computer, listed after **Everyone**. None of them can be written to until you share again.

### Share a slot

Drag a slot from the gallery onto the Chat panel, or right-click a slot and choose **Add to Chat** (this works even where you can only view, or on a frozen node). It waits in the message box as a small chip (choose its **×** to take it back out); add words if you like, then send. You can send a slot on its own.

In the conversation, a shared slot shows as a card with its picture and name. Choose the card to open the slot in the preview. Each person sees the slot from their own copy of the Flow, so someone whose share doesn't include that slot sees **Not available to you** instead, and its name is never shown to them. Guests can only share slots they can see.

### Send a picture or file

Choose the paperclip at the left of the message box, drag a file from your computer onto the Chat panel, or paste a picture into the box. Like a slot, it waits as a chip until you send, so you can add words first, and you can send a file on its own. There's no size limit; a big file takes as long as your connection needs. If a file can't be sent, nothing is sent, everything stays waiting, and a line under the box says which file it was.

In the conversation a picture shows itself and any other file shows its name and size. Choose either to save it to your computer. With **Save group chat with the Flow** on, pictures and files sent in **Everyone** are saved inside the Flow along with the messages, so the owner can still open them after sharing ends. Pictures and files in a private conversation are kept with your own copy of that conversation on your device, so they still open after the owner's computer has let go of them (for example once you've both downloaded them). They're never saved in the Flow, and a guest's copy goes when they leave the Flow. A file that's no longer anywhere says **No longer available**.

Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** to send. **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Web addresses (starting with http or https) become links.

### Reply and delete

Point at a message (or move to it with the keyboard) to see its small toolbar. **Reply** on someone else's message in Everyone opens your private conversation with them. **Delete** removes your own message; the owner can also **Delete for everyone** on any message in Everyone. Deleting always asks first, and can't be undone. A removed message leaves a short line saying who removed it.

### When the owner's FlowJoe is offline

If a guest's connection to the owner's FlowJoe drops, Chat stays open and everything already there stays readable, but nothing can be sent until it reconnects. Nothing is saved up to send later.

## What is saved, and where

A line above each conversation says what happens to it.

| Conversation | Where it is kept |
|---|---|
| **Everyone**, with **Save group chat with the Flow** on | In the Flow itself, so it travels with the Flow, its exports and its Workspace folder, and is there the next time you share. |
| **Everyone**, with saving off | New messages are kept nowhere: they're gone when sharing ends, and are never saved later. Turning saving off doesn't erase anything already saved with the Flow. |
| A private conversation | Never in the Flow. Each of the two people keeps a copy on their own device: the owner in FlowJoe on their computer, a guest in their browser. If a browser can't keep its copy (for example its storage is turned off), Chat says so in that conversation instead of promising a copy, and keeps trying. |

Messages between two guests pass through the owner's FlowJoe on the way; it doesn't show them to the owner. It holds them only until both guests' browsers have saved their own copy, and a picture or file on its way is kept locked (encrypted) so nothing readable stays on the owner's computer. A guest's copy is erased with everything else the share stored in their browser when they choose **Leave this Flow**, or when the link ends.

## Presenting

Chat is never shown to people following your presentation. Each person's Chat panel is their own, and a guest you have locked to your presentation can still open Chat and talk.
