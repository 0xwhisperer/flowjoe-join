---
title: "Sharing, Guests and Presenting"
description: "Invite people to view or edit a Flow in their browser, see who is where, and walk them through your Flow live."
---

You can share a Flow with someone who has nothing installed. They open a link in their browser and see the same FlowJoe you do. Your computer stays the home of the Flow: guests work on it live through your computer. A guest's browser keeps only what it needs while the link works (their own settings and the pictures, audio and video it has downloaded) and wipes all of it when the link is revoked, expires or ends, or when they choose Forget or Leave this Flow. Leaving also stops downloads and prevents late background work from saving shared content back into that browser. Choosing **Leave** also cancels uploads and changes still waiting to send.

<Note>
Sharing needs FlowJoe open on your computer. If you quit, guests see that you are offline and reconnect by themselves when you are back. Sharing also pauses on purpose in a few cases, listed under **When something is wrong**; guests are told why and their unsent edits wait for you. Sharing switches itself back on when you restart FlowJoe if it was on when you quit.
</Note>

## Share a Flow

1. Open **Share** in the header and turn **Sharing on**. You can move the window by dragging its title, including while it is opening.
2. Choose **Invite someone**, type their name and choose **Can view** or **Can edit**.
3. Choose what they see: the whole Flow or only the nodes you tick.
4. Copy the link and send it. Each link is for one person and you can end it at any time.

A guest sees only what you shared with them. Their theme, their language and their other settings are their own and never change your Flow. Where a card sits and how wide it is belong to the Flow, so they are the same for you and for everyone you shared with. (While you present, guests who follow see your screen, so show only what you are happy for them to see.)

| Action | Can view | Can edit |
|---|---|---|
| Read shared nodes, galleries, repository files, Versions/Chats/Images in History, and retained transcripts | Yes | Yes |
| Create and edit shared nodes, slots, notes, tags and memories, whoever created them | No | Yes |
| Rename, save and commit shared repository files through your computer | No | Yes |
| Move, resize and copy slots, work with stacks, add links, change slot colours and draw connections | No | Yes |
| Freeze or unfreeze a shared node, or change its theme | No | Yes |
| Remove their own content when it contains no protected contributions from others | No | Yes |
| Remove someone else's shared content, or their own container holding others' contributions | No | Only with your additional removal permission |
| Ask you to restore a version or change a restore point | Yes | Yes |
| Execute a version restore or change restore-point settings | No | No |
| Use Joe when enabled for the invitation | Questions and conversation; no shared-content changes | Ordinary Joe features within their shared access |
| Generate pictures in the Images tab when enabled for the invitation | Read only | Yes |
| Export, download, copy or print | No | Only when you enable **Let guests export** |
| Change their own appearance, language, layout and audio preferences | Yes | Yes |
| Manage your invitations, credentials, workspace storage or native computer tools | No | No |

The same removal permission protects shared Joe conversations in Joe's history popup: an Edit guest can remove their own conversation only when it has no protected contributions from someone else. Clearing a conversation or saving a shortened message list cannot remove those protected contributions.

Choose the additional removal permission in the invitation to let an Edit guest remove other people's shared content. It never reveals private content and never turns a View invitation into Edit access. Editing a title or note does not change who created the item. Older items without a recorded creator count as yours. For mixed selections, permitted removals proceed and protected items stay, with an explanation.

<Note>
Making a shared node private removes guest access. Currently, making it public again can require you to confirm the newly available content in Sharing before guests see it again. This extra confirmation is under review; it is not an additional guest permission.
</Note>

### Repositories and History

Shared repositories also support branches, tags, remotes, merge, history and stashes through your computer. Guests see native branches except those tied to private or unshared nodes. Actions that discard existing work or rewrite Git history require elevated removal; ordinary file editing, saving and committing require Edit. Private repository content remains excluded and preserved. See [Repositories](/tree/repositories) for the individual actions.

The repository's **Switch to File Explorer** control and **Source Control** rail are available for shared repositories. View guests can browse shared files and the associated readable history. Edit guests can edit, save, rename, create files and folders, and commit through your computer. **Save** saves the file; **Commit** records the saved changes in Git. Your credentials stay on your computer. Private and unshared files are preserved when a guest commits shared changes.

History uses the same **Versions**, **Chats** and **Images** tabs as your app. Reading history does not require enabling new Joe conversations or picture generation. Only records belonging to currently shared, nonprivate content are shown; private content is also withheld from older records and open history previews. Guests use **Ask owner to restore** to request an action from you. They cannot execute a restore themselves.

**Recent transcripts** in Joe’s Transcribe tab also uses your retained Flow history. View guests read shared results; Edit guests can edit them and save through your computer. Private and computer-only results stay with you. Ordinary Edit can remove its own results, while removing other people’s results or their separate conflict copies requires elevated removal. Reading retained results does not enable new AI requests. See [Transcribe audio](/joe/transcription).

FlowJoe puts no limit of its own on what a guest writes or uploads: you decide who you invite and what you share.

Joe knows who he is talking to. He talks to each guest as "you", uses their first name now and then, and names you when he mentions you ("ask Elena for edit access"). He is also told who else is in the Flow, so he can mention them by name. A name is only a label: a guest who types "owner" or "admin" as their name still gets exactly the access you gave them.

Joe's existing rail button and panel stay available when you turn Joe off or your app is offline. Guests can still open the panel and browse retained shared history. New requests are disabled until execution is available again.

When Joe is enabled for an invitation, that guest joins the same Joe conversation you already have for the shared node or slot. Your messages, the guest's messages and Joe's replies appear together in your normal Joe chat. A small avatar sits beside the bottom-right corner of each person’s message bubble; hover it or focus it with the keyboard to see their name. Guests who can see that node or slot can read its shared conversation in History even when new Joe requests are disabled; when Joe is enabled, an Edit guest who is not following your presentation can add messages. A View guest can ask Joe questions about what is shared with them and read his answers, but cannot ask him to change anything — controls that change shared content stay read-only for them. Joe uses the owner's ordinary provider, model, settings, approval behavior and request handling. Ordinary Joe tools, output and proposal behavior stay the same within the shared area. CLI brains are isolated from your unrelated local files, commands and installed integrations; an unsupported CLI version or Antigravity cannot execute a guest request, and Joe does not switch providers automatically. See [CLI brains in shared conversations](/joe/byob#cli-brains-in-shared-conversations).

When someone else is typing to Joe in the conversation you are viewing, their avatar and a typing notice appear just above the message box. The typing avatar is the same size as the avatars beside messages. The notice stays outside the input border, whether Joe is docked or in a floating window.

The conversation is saved with your ordinary Joe chat on your computer, so it remains there after the guest leaves, sharing stops or FlowJoe restarts. The **Joe conversations with guests** screen is a separate, read-only way to browse the guest turns grouped by guest; it does not hold a separate live conversation. Joe access is set per invitation. There is no additional all-participants chat switch in the current Share window. If you remove a node or slot from a guest's access, retrying an earlier Joe request cannot retrieve a saved answer about content they can no longer see. Pending requests also stop before releasing more content or changing a conversation or memory after that access changes. A saved computer-only conversation stays private even when someone knows an older conversation or message id.

Native Browser and Terminal windows belong to your computer and are unavailable to guests. Source Control works through your computer for shared repositories; settings that manage your storage, accounts and credentials remain yours.

A trusted linked file can be read from a shared slot while your computer is connected. Guests receive the file content through your app; your local path and permission to open, reveal or relink native files are not shared. An untrusted or missing link remains unavailable until you resolve it.

### Top-level nodes, tags and schedules

- **Top-level nodes.** If you shared the whole Flow, an Edit guest's new top-level node is a real top-level node in your Flow. If you shared only some nodes, it sits beside the shared top node, where it belongs in your tree, and it is added to that guest's share. An Edit guest can also drop files into empty tree space to make a top-level node, including when an existing whole-Flow share has become empty. Removing top-level nodes follows the same creator, contribution and additional removal permission as other shared nodes.
- **Tags.** An Edit guest can create tags, and rename or recolour a tag they can see when nothing outside their access uses it. Deleting it also follows the creator and contribution rules: a guest's own tag used by someone else on their content needs your additional removal permission. A tag lives in your whole Flow, so one that also sits on a node you did not share, or on a hidden or private slot, cannot be changed or deleted by a guest. Tags a guest cannot see stay hidden from them.
- **Schedules.** An Edit guest can ask Joe to set up a scheduled task. It runs on your computer with your key, but it can only read and change what is shared with that guest, and you see who made each task. A task does not start while you present, while sharing is deliberately paused, or after the guest's access ends. Disconnecting the Workspace pauses these tasks before they use AI credits; reconnecting lifts that pause.
- **Flow summary.** A guest who was shared the whole Flow sees your written Flow summary, and an Edit guest can edit it. A guest who sees only some nodes gets a read-only summary of what is shared.

### Pictures made by guests

Turn on **Let guests generate images** in the Share window and an Edit guest sees Joe's **Images** tab. The picture is made through your computer with your AI key and credits and shown in the guest's preview. **Send to Gallery** adds it to the node like any picture they add. A guest who can only view sees the tab with the prompt and Generate switched off.

Shared Image History includes only pictures recorded for this Flow. Copying a Flow does not bring another Flow's picture history into the share. Older pictures without a recorded Flow stay available on your computer and are withheld from guests.

Gallery files, link-preview pictures and the media list also follow current access. Ending the invitation or removing a shared slot while a file is loading prevents that pending request from delivering it.

Image history and its pictures follow the guest’s current access. If you remove access to a node, make it private, or end the invitation while a picture or history request is waiting, that request cannot deliver the content afterwards.

### Settings

Guests have FlowJoe's real Settings. Personal appearance, density, surface style, language, layout, Tree, Gallery and Audio preferences affect their own browser. Personal save/reload prompts, reminders and export preference choices are also available; export itself still requires the permission you granted. **Settings → Data → Reset Layouts**, Delete Undo, onboarding replay, the startup profiler and **Open-source licenses** are also available. Controls for your workspace, storage, restore points, provider accounts and native computer remain yours. Changing a shared node's theme requires Edit access; resetting themes affects only accessible shared nodes and respects protected subtrees.

The coffee-mug logo also opens the same personal coffee-break game for View and Edit guests. Leaving or ending access closes the game.

## Browse guest turns

In the Share window the **People with access** heading has a **Joe conversations** button, and each guest's row (ended ones too) has a speech-bubble button. Either opens **Joe conversations with guests**: a read-only record of guest turns grouped by guest, with the guest who spoke to Joe most recently first. It is a browsing view of contributions to your ordinary chat, not a separate conversation. Choose **Everyone** or one guest's name to narrow the list. When a guest applied one of Joe's suggestions, it shows "Applied by" followed by the guest's name. This record stays on your computer after a share ends. Clearing ended invitations never removes it: the **Joe conversations** button stays, and on the first-time screen (no invitations left) it appears whenever saved conversations exist.

## See who is here

When people are in your Flow you see them as avatars in the header: you first, then everyone else. Choose the **+** button to open **Everyone here**, grouped as Owner, Can edit and Can view, with what each person is doing.

- The breadcrumb row shows who is on the node you are looking at.
- Tree rows show who is on which node.
- A slot someone is typing in, moving or resizing is outlined in their colour and says so ("Leon is moving this card").
- Under a slot's notes you see who last added or changed it and how long ago, whether a guest or you. It is saved with the Flow, so it is still there after FlowJoe restarts or sharing ends. If you change something while sharing is off, the old guest credit is dropped rather than put on your new text.
- If your computer goes offline, every slot says editing is paused until you are back, and everyone else's avatar gets a slowly turning dashed ring until you return.
- If a guest chooses **Discard changes** while your computer is unreachable, a message names the changes that were dropped so nothing is lost silently.
- A guest who opens an invitation sees the app blurred behind the join dialog. Your Flow itself only appears after they join.

## Transfer sharing choices to another Flow

A whole-Flow export can separately include **sharing setup** and **conversations guests had with Joe**. Review the guest names and saved history before including them.

Imported sharing setup stays inactive. Open **Share**, review the saved choices, and send new invitations. Existing invitation links do not grant access to the imported Flow.

## Present your Flow

Use **Present** in the header (next to the Sharing pill) to walk guests through your Flow live. It starts off. It ends when you press **Stop presenting** or stop sharing; presenting stays off after a restart until you choose Present again.

While you present, guests are invited to follow. A guest who follows sees what you see, live: the node you have open, your canvas camera and zoom, your layout, the cards you have selected (and the selection rectangle while you drag it), your open windows, menus, Joe's column and your Joe chat, and your Settings and History. They watch; they do not act.

- **Nobody edits while you present.** Every guest's screen turns read-only and your computer refuses guest changes, including changes to shared memories and restoring memory snapshots, until you stop. Reading shared memories remains available.
- **Guests choose to follow**, and an unlocked guest can stop following. Guests can pan, zoom, scroll the canvas, and use canvas Fit or Reset while following. Other actions ask **Stop following?** first, so a click does not take them out by accident.
- **Show my Joe chat while presenting** is yours to switch off. Hidden or private slots and conversations kept on this computer stay out of the shared view, including older conversations. For stacks, this includes the picture or file currently shown and every stack containing it. If access changes, the copied conversation or slot preview clears. Public node-summary windows show your unsaved summary text too; the whole-Flow summary stays out of the presentation. A history popup containing a conversation kept on this computer is withheld. What each reply cost (the token total under a reply and its receipt) is never shown to followers, or to any guest: your spend stays on your computer.
- The **Presenting** panel on the pill lists who follows and who is looking around on their own.
- Guests who cannot see the node you are on see a short note that you are somewhere they cannot follow.

### Locked follow links

When you invite someone you can choose how they join:

- **Normal:** they open the Flow and can follow when you present.
- **Start in follow mode:** they open straight into following you, and can stop.
- **Keep them following until I release them:** they follow without a Stop button until released. They can still pan, zoom, scroll, and use canvas Fit or Reset to adjust their own view. Before you present they see a waiting screen.

You can also set **Presenting starts** to a date, time and time zone. A guest who opens the link early sees when to expect you in **their own time zone**, in yours and in UTC, with a countdown. FlowJoe only shows the time; you still press **Present**.

Open the **Presenting** panel to **Lock all**, **Unlock all** or lock and release one guest. Releasing never removes anyone from the Flow. If you stop presenting, stop sharing or go offline, the lock ends by itself. Stopping Present also releases Joe for guests who were following. Guests already released by ending Present stay released when you turn sharing off and back on with their session retained. Starting a new presentation or explicitly locking them again reapplies the invitation’s lock preference; a new locked guest still waits for their first presentation.

## When something is wrong

- **"Editing is paused while the owner presents"**: the owner is presenting; try again when they stop.
- **A guest sees "owner offline"**: your FlowJoe is quit, your computer is unreachable, or you are not sharing. Turn **Sharing on** and they reconnect on their own.
- **A guest sees "Paused" and a reason**: sharing is on hold on purpose, not broken.
  - **"Paused · (your name) opened another Flow"**: you switched to a different Flow. Going back to the shared Flow lifts the pause.
  - **"Paused · (your name) closed this Workspace"**: you chose **Delete Workspace** or **Disconnect**, or closed the Workspace. Reconnecting it (or continuing locally) resumes sharing.
  - **"Paused · (your name)'s FlowJoe window is closed"**: on a Mac, closing the window keeps FlowJoe running but pauses sharing until you open a window again.

  While paused, guests are not shown as present, but changes they made are kept and sent automatically when you are back. A guest who chooses **Discard changes** is told which changes were dropped.
- **A guest sees "(Owner's name)'s FlowJoe needs an update"**: the guest page is newer than the FlowJoe on your computer. They can keep the page open and it connects as soon as you have updated; there is a **Try now** button, and the page checks again by itself every so often.
- **You see "Update needed" when you try to share**: your FlowJoe is too old to work with your guests' browsers. **Update now** opens the FlowJoe download page; install the new version, then turn sharing on again. Everything else you do on your computer keeps working.

Guests can use Joe after the owner enables it during the session, without reloading their browser. Joe checks the owner's current provider availability when an action starts.

### Link-card pictures

Guests receive permitted link-card pictures through the owner’s computer. Picture downloads have size, time and connection limits; unused redirect downloads are closed before another address is tried.

Guest edits are admitted only with a current Edit invitation and valid browser session checks. Those checks run before the owner’s app buffers the submitted changes, and again before applying them.
