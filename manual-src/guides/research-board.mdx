## Workflow Steps

<Frame caption="Use the same three steps with your real sources and evidence.">
  <img src="/images/diagrams/research-workflow.svg" alt="Collect source material, annotate what it means and what remains uncertain, then synthesize with tags and node summaries." />
</Frame>

### Step 1: Create a Research Node
1. Select your root node and press `N` (or `Ctrl+N`) to add a new child node.
2. Press `F2` (or `Enter` when the tree has focus) and rename it to `Research Hub`.
3. Create more child nodes the same way for different sources (e.g. `Web Articles`, `PDF Papers`, `Competitive Audits`).

### Step 2: Import Reference Files
1. Open your system Finder/Explorer.
2. Select your PDFs, spreadsheets, or images and drag them directly onto the gallery surface of the `PDF Papers` node.
3. FlowJoe creates cards for each file. Double-click the PDF slots to read the articles inline.

### Step 3: Extract and Document Meaning
1. Open a PDF slot and write a brief summary in its notes, highlighting key citations.
2. Open **Node Summary** from the toolbar (or press `S` with the node selected) and write an executive summary of the branch.

### Step 4: Ask Joe for Analysis
1. Press `Cmd+Shift+A` / `Ctrl+Shift+A` to open the Joe panel.
2. Ask Joe: *"Read the selected PDF paper slot and summarize its primary findings in three bullet points."*
3. Use the reply’s **Send to Gallery** action, choose the slot’s notes, then confirm.
