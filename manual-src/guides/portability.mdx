---
title: "Data Portability & Backups"
description: "How to move a project to a new computer without losing files."
---

Follow this guide to safely transfer your visual workspaces and assets to a new machine or backup drive.

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Prepare the Export
1. Right-click the root node of the project you want to move.
2. Select **Export Subtree**.
3. Choose the **Full-Package Media Bundle (`.fj`)** format.
4. Save the file to your disk. FlowJoe compiles your tree database and packages all IndexedDB media files into the archive.

### Step 2: Transfer the Package
Copy the `.fj` package onto an external drive or send it to your target machine using local transfer tools.

### Step 3: Set up Preferences (Optional)
If you want your settings and playlists to match:
1. Open Settings on your original machine.
2. Click **Export Preferences**.
3. Transfer and import the preference file on the new machine.

### Step 4: Ingest on the New Computer
1. Open FlowJoe on the new machine.
2. Click **Import** from the top app menu.
3. Choose your `.fj` package.
4. Select **Merge at Root** or **Overwrite** to restore the project.
