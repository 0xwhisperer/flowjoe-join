Most database or note-taking tools force your thoughts into flat lists, rows, or linear documents. FlowJoe is built for the branching, visual reality of complex planning and creative thinking. 

Here are the primary ways users leverage FlowJoe’s split-hemisphere model, structured from **Professional & Technical** workflows to **Creative & Personal** disciplines:

---

## 💻 Professional & Technical Workflows

### Product Management & Agentic Engineering
For product teams, software developers, and AI agents developers.
* **Feature Launch Maps:** Branch outlines from a root launch (e.g. *Billing*, *Analytics*) down to specifications, checklist tasks, and UI mockups.
* **Workbench Islands:** Edit spreadsheet specifications, project calendars, or rich text specs in isolated workbench slots.
* **Agentic Workspaces:** Connect the **Agent Bridge (MCP)** to let terminal-based agents (like Claude Code or custom scripts) inspect your tree outline and propose draft roadmap changes for your approval.

### Scientific & Academic Research
For researchers, graduate students, and R&D lab groups.
* **Lab Notebooks:** Create child nodes for individual experiment runs, storing step-by-step protocols, calibration files, and lab notes.
* **Literature Mapping:** Ingest papers, PDFs, and citations. Link research sources to observed data figures in a single structured layout.
* **Dataset Indexes:** Point to massive local CSV/XLSX spreadsheets using **linked local files** to reference data without bloating your project size.

### Legal Case Files & Auditing
For litigators, estate planners, and compliance officers.
* **Case Folders:** Structure cases by client, witness, deposition, and exhibit.
* **Evidence Packets:** Store scan PDFs, court transcripts, timeline charts, and audio statements.
* **Security & Privacy:** Put confidential witness files or client records into **Private Nodes** to keep them out of Joe's context. When an older Flow is opened in a Workspace Folder, FlowJoe preserves private content in owner-only storage and verifies the redacted folder copy before saving resumes. Copies made before migration cannot be recalled; a retained backup that still contains private text gets the same placeholder treatment, and a backup too damaged to check is removed. See [Workspace Folders](/workspaces/folders).

## 🎙️ Media & Content Production

### Podcast Producers & Audio Storytellers
For podcast hosts, producers, sound designers, and narrative audio teams.
* **Visual Episode Boards:** Create a root node for the *Show*, branch into *Seasons*, and nest individual *Episodes*. Inside the episode node, attach guest bio PDFs, research links, and sponsor read copy sheets side-by-side.
* **Sonic Clip Stacks:** Stack intro stings, music cues, sound effects, ad reads, and recorded interview drafts inside a single slot to compare takes.
* **Persistent Workspace Playback:** Play raw interview files or episode draft mixes in the persistent global player, allowing you to listen to drafts uninterrupted while jumping around the tree outline to add edit notes or timestamps.
* **ID3 Tag Auto-Scraping:** Instantly parse incoming MP3/M4A clips to extract titles, artists, and embedded cover art, rendering them as card thumbnails so visual reference audio is instantly browsable.
* **Private recording transcripts:** Transcribe interviews and voice notes for free on your computer, even offline after setup. Choose plain text or timestamps; keep paid online transcription available when you need its speaker-label option. [Transcribe audio](/joe/transcription).
* **AI-Assisted Show Notes:** Paste interview transcripts into nodes and ask Joe to compile show notes, extract highlight quotes, suggest social media captions, or generate timestamp markers under your approval.

### Social Influencers & Content Creators
For YouTubers, TikTok creators, newsletter writers, and social media managers.
* **Visual Content Calendars:** Plan YouTube, TikTok, or newsletter schedules side-by-side using the built-in calendar workbench, setting platform labels (*#youtube*, *#tiktok*) and custom visual statuses.
* **Thumbnail & Hook Stacks:** A/B test thumbnail designs or script hooks by stacking variations in a single slot, keeping layout previews clean and comparative.
* **Sponsor & Campaign Boards:** Map sponsor campaigns, tracking media briefs, deliverables, visual assets, and analytics metrics side-by-side.
* **Platform-Aware Scripting:** Write script drafts using the rich document workbench. Ask Joe to generate platform-specific caption variants, write catchy hook options, or outline a repurposing matrix (turning a long video outline into 5 short script concepts).
* **Publishing Checklists:** Set up automated interval tasks using Joe Automation to scrape trending references or rotate reference thumbnail image assets.

## 🎨 Artistic & Story Writing

### Authors, Book Publishers & Comic Creators
For novelists, children's book creators, graphic novelists, and publishing editors.
* **Graphic Novel Issue Boards:** Structure graphic novel volumes as root nodes, branching down into chapters and pages (Page 1, Page 2, Page 3). Organize individual panels or page drafts in media slots.
* **Visual Panel Stacks (Art Pass Tracking):** Keep script drafts, layout thumbnails, penciled lines, ink passes, color revisions, and lettering versions stacked inside a single gallery slot. Use the scrubber to flip between stages of page production.
* **Children's Book Spread Layouts:** Map layout spreads as nodes, putting draft text side-by-side with mock illustrations in the split-hemisphere gallery to maintain layout, typography, and image cohesion.
* **Audio Read-Aloud Cadence:** Record read-aloud tests of draft pages. Play them in the persistent header player to verify rhythm and cadence while editing draft text inside a rich document workbench.
* **Book Launch & Cover Art Stacks:** Manage manuscript edits, cover mockups, distributor sheets, and promotional assets side-by-side, stacking different cover designs in one place to compare variants.
* **Character Bibles & Continuity:** Set up character rosters, location bibles, and model sheets. Use Joe to analyze dialogue passes, write panel description variants, or scan drafts for continuity leaks.

### Tabletop & Indie Game Design
For game developers, wargamers, and board game prototyping.
* **Balance Units:** Track units, points costs, and special rules using custom grid systems.
* **Playtest Logs:** Set up a dedicated playtest log branch to capture photos of prototype boards, audio session notes, and balance feedback.
* **AI Lore Hooks:** Call Joe directly inside a character node's slot to brainstorm flavor text or generate concept art references.

### Worldbuilding & Story Bibles
For authors, worldbuilders, and wargame narrative designers.
* **Organize Lore:** Set up a root node for the *Magic System*, *Geography*, or *Historical Timeline*, and branch down to individual characters, spell listings, or regional maps.
* **Mood Boards:** Drag and drop reference illustrations, character headshots, audio recordings, or clothing samples directly into the slots on relevant nodes.
* **Visual Stacks:** Keep multiple iterations of character portrait drafts or dialogue sheets stacked together in a single gallery slot.

## 🏡 Personal & Archival

### Personal Life Archives
For family histories, daily journaling, travel planning, or errands.
* **Family Tree Archives:** Build branches from grandparents to grandchildren, storing portraits, handwritten letter scans, and voice messages.
* **Travel Journals:** Group travel nodes with restaurant screenshots, hotel reservation PDFs, and mapped routing lists.
