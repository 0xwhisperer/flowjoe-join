Joe works beside your tree and references. You can brainstorm a project, make sense of a document, compare images, draft a summary, or ask him to turn a rough idea into an organized branch.

## From conversation to action

Choose a Flow, node, or slot as Joe’s target. Give him the material he needs, then ask a question or describe a change. For Flow edits, Joe offers a proposal you can approve, reject, or revise.

For example: “Group these research links into Sources and Findings, and write a short summary of each.” You review what he intends before it becomes part of the tree.

## More than chat

- Read documents, spreadsheets, calendars, and images together.
- Write and refine summaries or slot notes, with a review before replacing your text.
- Generate artwork and keep variations together in a stack.
- Organize nodes, tags, and gallery references.
- Save useful memories for later conversations.
- Set up recurring work, such as refreshing a research board.
- Control music while you work.

Chat edits normally need your approval. Scheduled tasks and connected agents can apply changes under permissions you choose. See [Proposals & approval](/joe/propose-approve) and [Automations](/joe/automation).

## Your choice of brain and context

Configure a supported API connection or signed-in command-line brain. Joe’s access controls let you choose whether he can read the Flow or propose changes. Private and Hidden-from-Joe content stay sealed; the outline may still show node names and positions.

Start with [Meeting Joe](/joe/opening-joe), [Bring Your Own Brain](/joe/byob), and [Permissions & privacy](/joe/permissions). To let an outside coding assistant work with the Flow, see [Agent Bridge](/joe/agent-bridge).
