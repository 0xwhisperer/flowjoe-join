Your thoughts, creative assets, and research drafts are valuable. They shouldn't be locked inside a closed cloud network or scanned by remote servers. 

FlowJoe is built as a **local-first desktop application**. Every document, image, audio file, and outline is stored directly on your computer.

---

## Why Local-First Matters

### ⚡ Blazing-Fast Performance
Because all assets live on your local drive, there are no loading spinners or network lag. Outlines open instantly, spreadsheets recalculate immediately, and large PDFs scroll smoothly.

### ✈️ True Offline Capability
FlowJoe does not require an active internet connection. You can outline your novel, edit budgets, and manage tasks on a flight, in a coffee shop with bad Wi-Fi, or deep in the mountains.

### 🔒 Absolute Privacy
Your data belongs to you. FlowJoe has no tracking servers. Your AI keys are stored locally in your operating system's secure keychain, and AI calls are made only when you explicitly ask Joe for assistance.

### 📁 Direct File System Compatibility
Unlike apps that lock data inside a single database file, FlowJoe's folder structure makes your project fully compatible with external tools. You can point your own backup scripts at it, or commit changes natively using Git.

---

## Portable & Open Data

You are never locked into FlowJoe. We make it easy to move, export, and copy your data:
- **Clipboard Portability:** You can right-click any node and copy its entire outline as clean JSON metadata to paste anywhere.
- **Data Backup:** Export your whole project as a lightweight `.json` structure or a full `.fj` package containing all your media assets zipped up.

<Accordion title="Technical Details: How Data is Structured Natively">
- **Tree and Text Data:** Serialized as JSON and saved locally in your Electron application user profile.
- **Media Blobs:** Indexed and stored in your device's local database (IndexedDB) for fast retrieval.
- **Workspace folders:** Workspaces map this structure directly to a folder on your filesystem (containing `flow.json` and a `media/` folder), making it compatible with Git and external backup systems.
</Accordion>
