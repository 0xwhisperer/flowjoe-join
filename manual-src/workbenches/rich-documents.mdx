The **Rich Document Workbench** utilizes Univer Docs to provide a real word-processing engine inside individual gallery slots.

---

## Workflows

- **Create Blank Document:** Click **Add Slot**, choose **New Document...**, then pick **Rich Text Document**.
- **Importing Text:** Drag and drop `.docx`, `.odt`, or `.rtf` files to import them. They open as editable document slots with styled text, lists, and embedded images; your untouched original stays hidden behind the slot so you can download it again.

- **Typography styling:** Change headers, font weights, colors, text alignment, and spacing.
- **Image Integration:** Paste images directly from your clipboard into the body of the document.
- **Headers & Footers:** Double-click the top or bottom margin of a page to edit its header or footer. Tables from imported files are kept when you save, but they cannot be edited in the editor yet.
- **Exporting:** Download your edited copy from the slot's menu. It always saves as a `.docx` file (`.odt` and `.rtf` files are imported but not written back).
- **Appearance:** The editor always uses a light look, even when the app is in a dark theme.

---

**Download Original** in the slot's menu returns the untouched imported file, with `_orig` before its source extension (for example, `Brief_orig.rtf`). It stays hidden behind the single editable slot, separate from the edited DOCX download.

## Collaborative AI Writing

Joe can work with your rich documents:
- **Content Retrieval:** Joe reads document text using the `get_island_content` tool.
- **Drafting and Editing:** Joe can read your document and suggest rewrites, spelling fixes, or expansions in chat, but he cannot write changes directly into a rich document yet. Copy the text you want into the editor.


Joe can read a late section by block and continue through a long document in pages, including stored headers and footers. If the document changes during a read, he must read it again rather than combine different versions.

When you edit a shared rich text document as an Edit guest, pictures you add are saved with the document on the owner’s computer. Later saves keep its existing pictures and imported original.


The Rich Document file-type badge appears beside the Preview title, leaving the editor toolbar clear.


## Saving and retrying

While editing, press **F6** (or **Ctrl+F6**) to return to Preview's **Close** control. This moves keyboard focus without closing the document. **Tab** and **Escape** keep their normal editor actions, and autosave continues as usual. The editor stays out of the way after you leave it, even if a cursor movement was still finishing. You can use Tab to return to the workbench controls or click the editing area to continue typing.

Closing Preview or moving to another stack item waits for your edits to save. If saving fails, the editor stays open with your draft. Once storage is available again, retry **Close**, **Save**, or the stack move without retyping your changes. An earlier autosave finishing does not cancel that retry: your latest changes still need to save. Keep the editor open until saving succeeds. Preview also waits for your latest edit when an earlier autosave is still finishing; it stays open until that edit is saved.

Closing the desktop window or quitting FlowJoe also waits for your editor to save. If saving cannot be confirmed, choose **Stay open** to keep your draft, or **Retry save** once storage is available again. **Close anyway** or **Quit anyway** leaves without confirming the save and may lose your unsaved changes.

When saving your edits, this also protects pictures you paste into the document: if a picture cannot be saved, your draft stays open for you to retry.


## Mac screen-reader access

Screen-reader reading and editing of document text is not yet verified on Mac. Current accessibility checks found that the built-in editor exposes toolbar controls but does not expose the tested document text through the Mac text-reading interfaces checked. This remains an open accessibility limitation. **F6** still moves focus from the editor to Preview’s **Close** control.
