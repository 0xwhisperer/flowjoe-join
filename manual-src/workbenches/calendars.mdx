---
title: "Calendar Workbench"
description: "Manage timelines, schedules, and ICS files in a unified view."
---

The **Calendar Workbench** integrates FullCalendar into a slot, letting you schedule, drag, and structure events side-by-side with research materials.

---

## Intakes & Imports

You can import schedules in several shapes:
- **Import ICS/iCal:** Drag-and-drop a `.ics` or `.ical` calendar file. FlowJoe displays a prompt letting you import it as:
  1. A **Calendar Slot** (interactive FullCalendar workbench).
  2. A **Stack** of event cards.
  3. Individual **Event Nodes** created under the selected parent.
- **New Blank Calendar:** Click **New Calendar** from the gallery menu.

---

## Interactive Timeline Editing

Double-click a Calendar slot to open the FullCalendar workbench:
- **Multiple Views:** Switch between Month, Week, Day, and Agenda List views.
- **Manage Events:** Create new events, rename titles, or delete events.
- **Drag & Resize:** Drag event cards to change dates, or drag block edges to resize duration. Changes write back to the underlying ICS format.
- **Exporting:** Save calendars back to a standard `.ics` file.

---

## Joe Integration

Joe receives calendar agendas as parsed, readable text:
- **Planning:** Ask Joe *"Find conflicts in my schedule next week"* or *"Draft an itinerary for Tuesday."*
- **ICS Preservation:** FlowJoe keeps the original source `.ics` file preserved in the slot metadata, allowing round-trips without data loss.
