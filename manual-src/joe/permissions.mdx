Joe can help at the level you choose—from a conversation about an idea to a reviewed reorganization of your Flow.

## Choose Joe’s access

Set the default in **Settings → Joe AI**. The selector in Chat overrides that default for the current conversation. Starting a new conversation clears the override.

| Level | What Joe can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | Chat and explain FlowJoe, without automatic access to your Flow or tools to read or change it. |
| **Read** | Read permitted Flow content to answer questions, without changing it. |
| **Propose** | Read permitted content and offer changes for you to review. |

These permissions apply to OpenAI, Gemini API and supported command-line brains. A brain may offer fewer features: [Antigravity](/joe/byob#which-brains-can-work-with-your-flow) does not support callable Flow tools or changes. These settings govern access through FlowJoe; installed command-line tools have their own computer-access permissions.

## Keep content out of Joe’s reach

- **Private nodes:** Their summaries, notes, gallery content, and descendants stay out of Joe’s Flow access, even while you unlock them for your own use.
- **Hidden from Joe:** Keeps a node’s contents and its descendants’ contents out of Joe’s access while leaving them available to you.
- **Names and contents are separate:** Joe can still see a sealed node’s name and position in the Flow map. This lets him understand where it belongs and refer to it in conversation without opening or changing its contents. Keep sensitive details out of node names.

These controls govern content Joe receives from the Flow. They cannot remove text you already pasted into a conversation or sent earlier.

## Ask about current app facts

With OpenAI API chat at **Read** or **Propose**, you can ask about the current storage mode, save blocks, export changes, pending conflict review, selected/responding model, Joe permissions, or a small performance sample. Joe reads only the requested facts. Unavailable measurements are marked as unavailable; an export-changes indicator does not prove that a local save failed or succeeded. No keys, local paths or diagnostic logs are included.

In a browser guest session, this reader can describe only the already available session model and permissions. Owner storage, save, conflict and computer diagnostics remain unavailable. New guest schedule inspection through Joe and transport parity are separate pending work; the existing shared Tasks lists are unchanged.

## Check the next message

Use **Add extra context** to choose gallery items for your next message. The target controls choose the Flow, node, or slot you are discussing. The token bar estimates how much context the send includes.

**Limit map to my location** narrows the outline. **Omit entire flow map** leaves the outline off that send. These are focus and cost controls; use Private or Hidden from Joe to seal content.
