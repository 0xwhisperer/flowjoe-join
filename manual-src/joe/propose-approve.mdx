Joe can do more than describe a better workspace: he can help you build it. Ask him to organize a branch, sort references, write notes, or arrange a board. His proposal always shows what will change before you decide, whichever brain you choose.

## Review a proposal

A proposal card lists additions, edits, and removals. When a proposal changes only notes, its heading stays short. Each notes comparison names its target and shows the complete **Before** and **After** text. Read both before applying; empty quotation marks mean the note is empty. These details stay with the saved outcome so you can review them after reopening the chat. The same note review is available with a CLI brain and stays available in the floating approval if Joe is closed or minimized. Long comparisons scroll so you can read the full text.

Choose:

- **Approve** to apply the listed changes. The card shows **Applying…**, then **Applied** or **Not applied**, with a reason.
- **Reject** to leave the Flow unchanged.
- **Revise…** to tell Joe what to adjust before he proposes again.

Rejected and revised cards leave a collapsed outcome in the conversation. You can dismiss the card separately. Outcomes stay with the saved chat.

In an eligible [private OpenAI chat](/joe/chat-analysis-generation#add-a-requirement-while-joe-is-replying), sending an update from the prompt replaces a waiting proposal. Its outcome says **Replaced by your update**, and Joe must prepare a new proposal for your approval. If you already chose Apply but the change has not started, the old proposal is not applied. A change that has already started finishes with its actual result; your update does not undo it.

If the Flow changed while a proposal was waiting, FlowJoe refuses the stale proposal rather than overwriting your newer work. With a CLI brain, reject that waiting card and ask Joe for a new proposal. Only the first proposal you decide on in a turn takes effect; Joe must re-check the Flow before offering more changes.

When a proposal removes a master slot that is shown in other places as [virtual slots](/gallery/virtual-slots#joe-and-virtual-slots), the card says you'll choose what happens to them. Approving opens the **Remove Master Slot** window first; Joe never makes that choice. A change to a master that is also shown in a frozen node names that node on the card.

## What you can ask Joe to change

With **Propose** access and a [brain that supports Flow tools](/joe/byob#which-brains-can-work-with-your-flow), Joe can carry out the actions below. Describe the result you want in ordinary language; you do not need to learn command names. He can combine several steps into one proposal—for example, building a branch, adding references, and arranging its cards together.

### Nodes and the tree

- **Build a structure:** create a root, child, sibling, or a whole nested branch.
- **Update it:** rename nodes, write their summaries, change status, apply a Theme-menu look, and expand or collapse branches. Changing a parent’s status also changes its descendants’ statuses.
- **Reorganize:** move one or several nodes, duplicate a branch, or sort siblings by name or the numbers in their names, in either direction.
- **Remove:** delete one node or several nodes, including their branches.
- **Control visibility and editing:** hide or show nodes in the tree, hide a branch from Joe, or turn Freeze on or off. Ordinary tree hiding is separate from **Hidden from Joe**. Only you can reveal a branch hidden from Joe; an approved proposal cannot do that for him.

Try: “Create a Research branch with Sources and Findings underneath it, then alphabetize its children.”

### Gallery cards and their contents

- Add cards using supported text, links, or media already supplied or prepared for the Flow; replace a card’s contents.
- Copy cards within a node or to another node, move one or several cards between nodes, reorder them, or sort by title or numbers in their titles.
- Edit card titles and notes, including the text attached to a stack or one of its items. Joe can also change an audio item’s displayed title.
- Delete selected cards or selected occurrences of an item. For cropped pictures, he can separate cropped copies from their original before removing the original, so those copies remain.

Editing a card’s notes is different from rewriting an attached file. These actions do not give Joe a general editor for every document or file type. Files managed by a [node repository](/tree/repositories) also have their own protections.

### Stacks

[Stacks](/gallery/stacks) let you keep alternatives, versions, and related references together without filling the gallery with separate cards. Joe can:

- Merge two cards, several selected cards, or a node’s whole gallery into one stack.
- Add items to a card, insert them at a chosen position in a stack, reorder items, or choose which item is displayed.
- Copy an item into its own card while leaving it in the stack, or move it out into this node or another node.
- Split **every** item into separate cards, keeping them all.
- Remove selected items, or **flatten** a stack to keep only its displayed item and remove the others.

<Warning>
“Split the stack” and “keep only this version” have different results. Flattening removes the other stack items. Check the removal list before approving.
</Warning>

### Pictures, audio, and canvas boards

- Crop a stored picture in place.
- Make a new PDF from a picture or note, keeping the source.
- Set or clear an audio item’s cover art using a picture already stored in FlowJoe.
- Set or clear a canvas card’s color, move it to a position, set its width, or restore its usual width.
- Connect two cards on the same board, or remove their connection.

Try: “Put the three reference cards in a row, make the middle card wider, and connect the first card to the second.”

Joe can also [generate or edit a picture](/joe/chat-analysis-generation#image-generation) with your OpenAI connection, then propose where to place it. Generating the picture and approving its placement are separate steps.

### Spreadsheets and other workbenches

Joe can read a spreadsheet and propose changes to individual cells, including several cell changes in one proposal. Each change writes a **plain value**. If that cell held a formula, the new value replaces the formula.

He cannot write spreadsheet formulas, format cells, add rows or sheets, or edit a rich document or calendar through these proposals. He can read supported rich documents and calendars to help you understand or plan their contents.

### Tags and Flow details

Joe can create, rename, update, or delete tags and assign, replace, or clear tags on nodes and cards. A stack’s tags apply to the whole stack. He can also change the Flow’s title and summary.

Try: “Create a ‘To review’ tag and put it on these reference cards.” Creating and assigning the tag can be one proposal.

### Memories and recurring tasks

Joe can create a [saved memory](/joe/memories-compaction), enable or disable an existing memory, or delete it. To rewrite an existing memory’s text directly, use the Memories panel.

He can create or update a [scheduled task](/joe/automation), pause or resume it, or delete it. Updating a task does not move it to a different node. A task’s schedule, target, and approval settings determine how its later runs work. Creating a recurring task is a separate decision from approving a one-time chat change.

## What else Joe can help with

Changes are only part of Joe’s toolkit. Depending on your brain, access settings, and available services, he can also:

| Ask Joe to… | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Find something in the Flow | He can inspect the permitted outline, nearby and related nodes, and search node names, summaries, and card or stack-item titles and notes. This search does not search inside every attached document. |
| Read a reference | He can open supported text and Markdown notes, PDFs, Word, ODT, PowerPoint, and EPUB files, or inspect spreadsheet, rich-document, and calendar contents. Large results may be shortened. |
| Look at an image | He can inspect an accessible gallery picture or look again at an image attached to the current chat. A picture’s title alone does not tell him what is in it. |
| Find pictures by what they show | With a [TypeSafe API key](/joe/token-usage#the-optional-typesafe-key), he can rank the pictures in part of your Flow by what they show—“the warm, minimal interiors in my moodboard”, “more like this one”—then open only the best matches. This works in your own desktop chat on every brain that uses Flow tools (OpenAI, Gemini, and CLI brains such as Claude, Grok and Codex); assistants connected from outside cannot use it. On a Mac this includes Antigravity; on Windows and Linux, Antigravity can't use Flow tools yet. Without a key, or if TypeSafe can’t help, he picks pictures by their names. |
| Help with a file you have mentioned | He can show a **Choose file** request. You choose whether to supply it; a chosen file is imported into the current node’s gallery. |
| Prepare media | He can write a text or Markdown file, create SVG artwork, prepare supplied media, or generate a picture for a later placement proposal. This does not let him load arbitrary remote image, video, or audio addresses into your gallery. |
| Take you to a node | He can select the node and show its gallery immediately. A node link in his answer lets you choose when to go there yourself. |
| Play audio | He can load a playlist or a node’s audio, play one track once, pause, change playback mode, play tagged audio, or save a new named playlist. These controls act immediately; see [Global audio player](/gallery/audio-player). |
| Explain what he remembers or has changed | He can inspect saved memories and the decided proposals in **this conversation**, including rejected or revised proposals. This is separate from all-app version history. |
| Hand you a restore | He can find a saved version and show a **Restore a saved version** card. You press **Review restore** and confirm in the usual History dialog; he never restores by himself. See [Ask Joe to restore a version](/workspaces/recovery#ask-joe-to-restore-a-version). |
| Hand you an export | He can show an **Export the selected node** card. You press **Review export** and choose the format, options, password and save location in the usual Export dialog; he never exports by himself and never sees the file. See [Ask Joe to export a node](/portability/export-import#ask-joe-to-export-a-node). |
| Hand you an import | He can show an **Import a Flow** card. You press **Choose import file**, pick the file and choose the import mode in the usual File menu import; he never imports by himself and never sees the file. See [Ask Joe to import a Flow](/portability/export-import#ask-joe-to-import-a-flow). |
| Hand you the outside-changes review | He can show a **Review changes from another computer** card. You press **Review outside changes** and decide in the usual review; he never chooses a resolution. See [Ask Joe to review outside changes](/workspaces/recovery#ask-joe-to-review-outside-changes). |
| Check recurring work | He can list scheduled tasks and their reported next run, last result, and current state. |
| Explain FlowJoe | He can consult the app’s built-in reference chapters when he needs more detail. |

Web search and Code interpreter are separate options, with availability depending on the selected brain. See [Bring Your Own Brain](/joe/byob). A [connected external assistant](/joe/agent-bridge) uses the same supported change actions through MCP, with its own connection, approval, and history controls.

Want the technical name of each action? The [CLI action reference](/cli/action-reference) lists them, with examples. It also notes the few things [only Joe’s chat can do](/cli/action-reference#only-in-joes-chat).

## Undo a change

Use **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** after an approved change, or ask Joe to undo his last chat change. Chat undo only reverses Joe’s own chat edits. Scheduled runs and connected agents have their own undo controls.

Files removed by an undoable change are retained while undo may need them, so restoring a card can restore its media too.

## Access and automatic changes

Private content stays sealed from Joe even when you unlock it for yourself. Hidden-from-Joe content is also sealed; node names and positions may still appear in the outline. Freeze blocks most changes to a node and its gallery. Joe can still update its summary or propose turning Freeze off; a frozen ancestor continues to protect its children. A duplicate starts unfrozen, unless its parent is still frozen.

Joe’s chat proposals always need your approval, on every brain. Agent Bridge’s session **Apply changes without asking** setting only affects outside assistants, never Joe chat. [Scheduled tasks](/joe/automation) and [connected agents](/joe/agent-bridge) can apply changes under permissions you choose. Review their settings before authorizing unattended work.

Joe’s audio playback controls take effect immediately without a proposal card. See [Global audio player](/gallery/audio-player).

## Links Joe adds

In **Settings → Joe AI → Link previews for cards Joe adds**, choose **Load automatically** or **Wait for a click**. The second choice shows a **Load preview** button instead of fetching the page immediately. It also applies to scheduled tasks and connected agents. Links you add yourself load their previews normally.

Joe cannot add media that automatically loads a picture, video, or audio file from an arbitrary website. Generated or supplied media can be placed in the gallery. FlowJoe also rejects addresses Joe writes that carry your Flow’s own text inside the URL.
