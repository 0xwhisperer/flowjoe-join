title: "Proposals & approval"
description: "Turn Joe’s ideas into changes you can review, revise, and undo."
---

Joe can do more than describe a better workspace: he can help you build it. Ask him to organize a branch, sort references, write notes, or arrange a board. His proposal shows what will change before you decide.

## Review a proposal

A proposal card lists additions, edits, and removals. Choose:

- **Approve** to apply the listed changes. The card shows **Applying…**, then **Applied** or **Not applied**, with a reason.
- **Reject** to leave the Flow unchanged.
- **Revise…** to tell Joe what to adjust before he proposes again.

Rejected and revised cards leave a collapsed outcome in the conversation. You can dismiss the card separately. Outcomes stay with the saved chat.

If the Flow changed while a proposal was waiting, FlowJoe refuses the stale proposal rather than overwriting your newer work. Only the first proposal you decide on in a turn takes effect; Joe must re-check the Flow before offering more changes.

## What you can ask Joe to change

- Build, rename, move, sort, and organize nodes.
- Add, copy, sort, or remove gallery items; edit titles and notes.
- Combine references into stacks, choose the displayed item, or copy one item out.
- Create and assign tags, save memories, and set up recurring tasks.
- Apply a node’s Theme-menu look, color a canvas card, or connect two cards on the same board.
- Crop a picture, turn a picture or note into a PDF, or change audio cover art using an image already in FlowJoe.

For example: “Create a Research branch with Sources and Findings underneath it, then move these references into Sources.” Review the result before approving.

## Undo a change

Use **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** after an approved change, or ask Joe to undo his last chat change. Chat undo only reverses Joe’s own chat edits. Scheduled runs and connected agents have their own undo controls.

Files removed by an undoable change are retained while undo may need them, so restoring a card can restore its media too.

## Access and automatic changes

Private content stays sealed from Joe even when you unlock it for yourself. Hidden-from-Joe content is also sealed; node names and positions may still appear in the outline. Frozen nodes refuse content edits.

Chat proposals need your approval. [Scheduled tasks](/joe/automation) and [connected agents](/joe/agent-bridge) can apply changes under permissions you choose. Review their settings before authorizing unattended work.

Joe’s audio playback controls take effect immediately without a proposal card. See [Global audio player](/gallery/audio-player).

## Links Joe adds

In **Settings → Joe AI → Link previews for cards Joe adds**, choose **Load automatically** or **Wait for a click**. The second choice shows a **Load preview** button instead of fetching the page immediately. It also applies to scheduled tasks and connected agents. Links you add yourself load their previews normally.

Joe cannot add media that automatically loads a picture, video, or audio file from an arbitrary website. Generated or supplied media can be placed in the gallery. FlowJoe also rejects addresses Joe writes that carry your Flow’s own text inside the URL.
