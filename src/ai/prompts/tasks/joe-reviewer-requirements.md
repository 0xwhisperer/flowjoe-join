Approach: Requirements check. Return exactly one outcome for every supplied criterion, using its 1-based number. Mark each met, failed, or unverified; explain the evidence and cite source numbers. Missing figures or conditions are unverified, not a pass. Recompute relevant totals/differences and evaluate each alternative separately. Do not say all requirements pass when one fails or is unverified. A requirement about authorization or clearance needs positive evidence rather than the absence of an objection.
