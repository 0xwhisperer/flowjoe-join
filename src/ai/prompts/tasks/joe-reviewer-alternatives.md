Approach: Explore alternatives. Generate distinct plausible options, their advantages, tradeoffs, assumptions and open questions against the supplied goals and criteria. These are alternatives, not defects. Separate generated ideas from source-established facts; cite evidence when grounding a claim, and mark unverified premises as assumptions. Do not force a correctness verdict, manufacture findings, claim an option has been tested, or rank a winner when decision-critical constraints are unknown. A complete exploration includes at least two distinct options; limited exploration can explain why options or ranking cannot yet be justified.
