Internal Architecture & Product Proposal

Why the Boundaries Are Drawn Here

This mockup demonstrates a hybrid local-first business model inspired by Obsidian and Figma. The single-player application remains completely uncompromised, while real-time multiplayer networking acts as the monetization bridge.

1. Solo Creator ($0)

Preserves User Trust: 100% offline, zero forced logins. Workspace Folders & plugins are free. Includes a collaboration taste (1 guest for 30-minute review sessions) so solo users experience real-time sync with zero risk.

2. FlowJoe Pro ($8/mo)

The Conversion Trigger: The host pays when they want to share or present. Includes automated outbound relay tunnels (no router configuration), a 7-day free trial (no credit card), and free browser guest access.

3. Team & Studio ($16/seat)

Organizational Seats: Multiple permanent hosts who can each spin up tunnels, centralized company license billing, edge relay bandwidth, and shared studio AI quotas.

💡 Key Question 1: Are Pro Guests View-Only, or Can They Edit for Free?

Recommendation: Pro guests should definitely be able to EDIT for free (up to 3 concurrent editors per session).
3 concurrent editors (Host + 2 guest reviewers) covers 95% of real freelance/consulting jobs (e.g., Host + Client + Editor) without giving away an entire 5-person indie studio license for $8/month. Guests join free directly in their browser without an account. Only the host who owns the project and provisions the tunnel needs a paid Pro license.

🔒 Key Question 2: Why 7 Days, and How Do We Stop Burner Trials?

7 days is the conversion sweet spot: 14 days gives users enough time to finish a whole client project for free and forget about the app. 7 days covers one active work week, creates urgency, and drives higher conversions while cutting server relay exposure in half.

  • 1. Hardware / Device ID Locking (Primary Shield): When a trial activates, the desktop app registers the machine's hardware UUID with your auth server. Even if a user creates 10 different Gmail accounts, that specific computer is locked to exactly one 7-day trial per year.
  • 2. Subdomain Friction: Pro users get a personal address (e.g. dan.flowjoe.app). If a freelancer rolls burner accounts, their URL changes weekly and all previously shared client links break permanently. Professional reputation protects against trial-hopping.
  • 3. Zero Data Hostage: When the 7 days expire, no local work is locked. The user keeps all nodes, workbenches, and media on their hard drive. Only the outbound sharing tunnel pauses, making upgrading to $8/month an easy, unresented decision.

How FlowJoe Collaboration Works Behind the Scenes

Signing up for FlowJoe Pro automatically provisions a secure outbound encrypted tunnel and your personal address (e.g. dan.flowjoe.app or join.flowjoe.app/dan). Zero router fussing, zero port forwarding, and no complex firewall rules. Your flow data stays on your machine, streamed securely to your guests' browsers only when you open a session.
