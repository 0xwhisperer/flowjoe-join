Internal Architecture & Product Proposal

Reasoning

This mockup demonstrates a hybrid local-first business model inspired by Obsidian and Figma. The single-player application remains completely uncompromised, while real-time multiplayer networking acts as the monetization bridge.

1. Solo Creator ($0)
  • 100% offline and private.
  • No account or login required.
  • Workspace Folders and plugins stay free forever.
  • Includes short 30-minute review links to taste live sync.
  • Preserves user trust and builds organic word-of-mouth.
2. FlowJoe Pro ($8/mo)
  • The natural upgrade point when sharing with clients.
  • Provisions an automated outbound tunnel with no router setup.
  • Includes a custom personal address (e.g. dan.flowjoe.app).
  • Guests join free in their browser without an account.
  • Includes a risk-free 7-day trial with no credit card required.
3. Team & Studio ($16/seat)
  • Built for business continuity and team coordination.
  • Every team seat can host sessions and share links.
  • Centralized billing to add or reassign licenses.
  • Pools company AI keys with usage visibility.
  • Transfer project ownership so flows never get orphaned.
💡 Key Question 1: Are Pro Guests View-Only, or Can They Edit for Free?
  • Pro guests should edit for free, capped at 3 live editors per session.
  • Charging per guest creates high friction for simple client reviews.
  • Three editors covers the classic freelance team: Host, Client, and Reviewer.
  • Guests join free in their browser without creating an account.
  • The 3-editor cap prevents entire studios from sharing a single host license.
🔒 Key Question 2: Why 7 Days, and How Do We Stop Burner Trials?
  • 7 days creates urgency: 14 days lets users complete an entire job for free. 7 days covers one active work week and cuts server exposure in half.
  • Device ID locking: The desktop app logs the machine's hardware UUID. Each computer receives exactly one trial per year, blocking new Gmail accounts.
  • Subdomain friction: Rolling new accounts changes the user's link every week. Broken client links discourage professionals from trial-hopping.
  • No data hostage: Local files and nodes remain 100% accessible when the trial ends. Only the outbound tunnel pauses, keeping upgrades positive.

How FlowJoe Collaboration Works Behind the Scenes

Signing up for FlowJoe Pro automatically provisions a secure outbound encrypted tunnel and your personal address (e.g. dan.flowjoe.app or join.flowjoe.app/dan). Zero router fussing, zero port forwarding, and no complex firewall rules. Your flow data stays on your machine, streamed securely to your guests' browsers only when you open a session.
