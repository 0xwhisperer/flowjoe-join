Internal Architecture & Product Proposal

Why the Boundaries Are Drawn Here

This mockup demonstrates a hybrid local-first business model inspired by Obsidian and Figma. The single-player application remains completely uncompromised, while real-time multiplayer networking acts as the monetization bridge.

1. Solo Creator ($0)

Preserves User Trust: 100% offline, zero forced logins. Users own their data in flow.json and local IndexedDB. Word-of-mouth grows organically because solo power users are never nagged with paywalls.

2. FlowJoe Pro ($12/mo)

The Conversion Trigger: The host pays when they want to share or present. Includes automated outbound relay tunnels (no router configuration) and free browser guest access (both View and Edit).

3. Team & Studio ($24/seat)

Organizational Seats: Multiple permanent hosts who can each spin up tunnels, centralized company license billing, edge relay bandwidth, and shared studio AI quotas.

💡 Key Question: Are Pro Guests View-Only, or Can They Edit for Free?

Proposal: Pro guests should definitely be able to EDIT for free (up to 5 concurrent editors per session).
Charging per-guest (the Canva trap) introduces huge friction when a freelancer or consultant just wants client review notes. Instead, guests join free directly in their browser without an account. Only the host who owns the project and provisions the tunnel needs a paid Pro license. The concurrency cap prevents companies from abusing a single host license.

How FlowJoe Collaboration Works Behind the Scenes

Signing up for FlowJoe Pro automatically provisions a secure outbound encrypted tunnel and your personal DNS address (e.g. dan.flowjoe.link). Zero router fussing, zero port forwarding, and no complex firewall rules. Your flow data stays on your machine, streamed securely to your guests' browsers only when you open a session.
